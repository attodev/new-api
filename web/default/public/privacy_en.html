Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026  |  Atto Research, Inc.

Atto Research, Inc. (hereinafter "Company") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy pursuant to Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of Korea, to protect the personal information of data subjects and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly. This Policy is incorporated by reference into the Data Processing Addendum (DPA) of the Enterprise Service Terms of Use.

Article 1 — Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information processed will not be used for any purpose other than those listed below. If the purpose changes, we will take necessary measures such as obtaining separate consent pursuant to Article 18(2)(1) of PIPA.

Article 2 — Personal Information Processed

① The Company processes the following personal information:

  1. Required information: User ID, email address
  2. Automatically collected information: Service usage records, access IP, cookies, access logs (request timestamp, status code, API key identifier, etc. — processed only to the extent not combined with personal information)

② The Company collects only the minimum personal information necessary and does not collect sensitive information as defined under Article 23 of PIPA.

Article 3 — Retention and Use Period

① The Company processes and retains personal information within the retention period stipulated by law or agreed upon at the time of collection.

② Retention periods for each category of personal information are as follows:

  1. Member registration and management: Until contract termination. However, if an investigation or inquiry related to a violation of applicable law is ongoing, until such investigation or inquiry concludes.
  2. E-commerce payment and billing records: 5 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, Article 6)
  3. Communication records: 12 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act, Article 15-2)
  4. Service operations and incident response (API call timestamp, model used, response status code): 1 year after contract termination

③ When personal information becomes unnecessary due to expiration of the retention period or achievement of the processing purpose, it will be destroyed without delay.

Article 4 — Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

① The Company processes personal information only within the scope specified in Article 1 and provides personal information to third parties only with the data subject's consent or as permitted under Articles 17 and 18 of PIPA.

② At the user's request, prompts (text) entered by the user and call metadata (request identifier, call option parameters) are provided to and transferred internationally to the LLM Provider selected by the user.

Article 5 — Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

① The Company does not currently entrust personal information processing to any third party.

② If entrustment becomes necessary in the future, the Company will specify required safeguards in the contract with the processor pursuant to Article 26 of PIPA, and will manage and supervise the processor's handling of personal information.

③ Any changes to the processor or scope of entrusted work will be reflected in an updated Policy published without delay.

Article 6 — Processing of Customer Content

① Rights to inputs submitted by users to the Service and outputs generated by the Service (collectively "Customer Content") belong to the user.

② The Company does not store or collect Customer Content (prompt content or response content). Prompts entered by users are transmitted directly to the external AI model provider selected by the user and are not stored on the Company's servers.

③ However, the Company collects and stores the following API call logs (metadata) for service operations and quality management:

Purpose Data Collected Retention Period
Service operations and incident response API call timestamp, model used, response status code 1 year after contract termination
Billing processing and dispute resolution Token usage, billing records 5 years pursuant to Article 6 of the E-Commerce Consumer Protection Act

④ The logs in paragraph ③ do not include prompt content, and the Company does not use them for any purpose other than service provision.

⑤ Processing of personal information within Customer Content is governed by the privacy policy of the external AI model provider selected by the user. The external AI model provider acts as an independent data controller and bears responsibility under its own privacy policy for personal information processed on its servers.

Article 7 — Destruction of Personal Information

① The Company destroys personal information without delay when it becomes unnecessary due to expiration of the retention period or achievement of the processing purpose.

② Where personal information must continue to be retained under applicable law despite expiration of the agreed retention period or achievement of the processing purpose, the relevant personal information will be moved to a separate database or stored separately.

③ The procedures and methods for destruction are as follows:

  1. Procedure: The Company identifies personal information subject to destruction and destroys it with the approval of the Privacy Officer.
  2. Method: Personal information stored in electronic files is destroyed in a manner that prevents recovery. Personal information recorded in paper documents is shredded or incinerated.

Article 8 — Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

① Data subjects may exercise the following rights against the Company at any time:

  1. Request to access the status of personal information processing
  2. Request to correct erroneous personal information
  3. Request to delete personal information
  4. Request to suspend processing of personal information

② Rights under paragraph ① may be exercised by written request, email, or facsimile to the Privacy Officer, and the Company will take action without delay.

③ Rights may also be exercised through a legal representative or duly authorized agent. In such cases, a power of attorney in the form prescribed by Annex 11 of the Enforcement Rules of PIPA must be submitted.

④ The right to request access to or suspension of processing of personal information may be restricted pursuant to Articles 35(4) and 37(2) of PIPA.

⑤ Requests to correct or delete personal information that is specified as a collection target under another statute cannot be made.

⑥ The Company verifies that the person making a request for access, correction/deletion, or suspension of processing is the data subject or a lawful representative.

Article 9 — Measures to Ensure Security of Personal Information

The Company implements the following technical, administrative, and physical measures necessary to ensure security pursuant to Article 29 of PIPA.

① Administrative measures

  1. Establishment and implementation of an internal management plan
  2. Regular privacy training for employees
  3. Minimization of staff handling personal information and management of access privileges

② Technical measures

  1. Management of access rights to personal information processing systems and password security
  2. Installation and regular updating/inspection of security programs against hacking and other breaches
  3. Encryption of personal information: passwords and other critical information are encrypted for storage and transmission
  4. Retention of access records and measures to prevent tampering (retained for at least 1 year)

③ Physical measures

  1. Access control for server rooms and data storage facilities

Article 10 — Installation, Operation, and Opt-Out of Automatic Data Collection Devices

① The Company uses cookies that store and retrieve usage information to provide personalized services to users.

② Cookies are small pieces of information transmitted by the server operating the website to the user's computer browser, where they are stored on the user's hard disk.

③ Users may allow, block, or delete cookies through their web browser settings. However, refusing to store cookies may limit use of certain services.

Article 11 — Electronic Communications

① The Company may send users the following electronic communications by email, in-service notifications, or website posting for the purpose of performing the service agreement or fulfilling legal notification obligations:

  • Service-related notices (account information, usage history, outage notices, etc.)
  • Notifications required by law, such as changes to terms or service suspension
  • Payment and billing notices

② When sending electronic communications for marketing or promotional purposes, the Company obtains separate consent from users, distinct from the consent in paragraph ①, pursuant to Article 22(1)(7) of PIPA.

③ Users may withdraw consent to receive marketing or promotional communications at any time. Withdrawing such consent does not restrict use of the Service.
